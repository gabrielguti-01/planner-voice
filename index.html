                Spotify check

                  Spotify says "offline"?
                  1. In Spotify: tap your picture → Settings and privacy → Playback → turn Offline mode off.
                  2. iPhone Settings → Spotify → turn on Cellular Data and Background App Refresh.
                  3. Turn off any VPN or ad blocker (Settings → VPN, and Settings → General → VPN & Device Management).
                  4. Settings → Screen Time: make sure Spotify has no App Limit and isn't blocked by Downtime.
                  5. Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically on (a wrong clock breaks secure connections).
                  6. Still offline: in Spotify, Log out and log back in. Last resort: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Spotify → Offload App, then tap it again to reinstall.
                  7. Then open Spotify, play any song for a few seconds, come back here and tap Check Spotify.
